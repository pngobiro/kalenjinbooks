# KaleeReads Platform - User Guide

## What is KaleeReads?

KaleeReads is an online library and publishing platform dedicated to Kalenjin books, stories, and cultural heritage. Whether you're a reader looking for authentic Kalenjin literature or an author wanting to share your work, KaleeReads connects you with a community that values our rich cultural traditions.

---

## For Readers

### Browsing Books

1. **Explore the Catalog**
   - Visit the Books page to see all available titles
   - Use the search bar to find books by title, author, or topic
   - Filter by category (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Folklore, History, Poetry, Children's books, Education)

2. **Book Details**
   - Click on any book to see its full description
   - View the author's profile and other works
   - Check the price and preview pages available
   - Read reviews and ratings from other readers (Coming Soon)

3. **Reading Books**
   - Purchase a book to get temporary access (7 days)
   - Read directly in your browser - no downloads needed
   - Your active books are available in your account

**Coming Soon:**
- Rate and review books you've read
- Follow your favorite authors for updates
- Get notifications when followed authors publish new books

### Requesting Physical Copies

If you prefer a printed book:
1. Go to the "Request Hard Copy" page
2. Fill in your contact information
3. Specify which book you want and how many copies
4. The author will contact you directly to arrange delivery and payment

## For Authors

### Getting Started

1. **Sign In**
   - Click "Author Dashboard" on any page
   - Sign in with your Google account
   - First-time users will see a registration form

2. **Register as an Author**
   - Fill in your author profile (name, bio, contact information)
   - Submit your application
   - Wait for admin approval (you'll receive an email notification)

### Publishing Your Books

Once approved as an author:

1. **Create a New Book**
   - Go to your Author Dashboard
   - Click "My Books" then "Add New Book"
   - Fill in the book details:
     - Title and description
     - Category and language
     - Price (what readers will pay)
     - Upload a cover image
     - Upload your book file (PDF format)

2. **Book Status**
   - After submission, your book goes to admin for review
   - You'll receive an email when it's approved
   - Approved books appear in the public catalog
   - You can edit or unpublish your books anytime

### Managing Your Content

**My Books**
- See all your published and draft books
- Edit book details or pricing
- View how many people have read your books
- Disable books temporarily if needed

**Hard Copy Requests**
- See who wants physical copies of your books
- Contact information is provided for each request
- Arrange delivery and payment directly with readers

**Analytics**
- Track how many people view your books
- See which books are most popular
- Monitor your earnings over time

**Earnings**
- View your total revenue
- See earnings per book
- Track payment history

### Your Author Profile

Keep your profile updated:
- Add a professional photo
- Write an engaging bio
- Include your contact information
- Link to your social media or website

## Payment & Pricing

### For Readers
- Prices are set by individual authors
- Books are available for temporary access (168 hours/7 days)
- No subscription fees - pay only for books you want

**Payment Methods (Under Development)**
- M-Pesa integration coming soon
- Credit/Debit card payments
- Other mobile money options

Note: Payment features are currently being developed. Check back soon for updates on how to purchase books.

### For Authors
- You set your own book prices
- For physical copies, you handle payment directly with readers

**Important: Payment Model Decision Needed**

The platform owner needs to decide on the payment structure:

**Option 1: Direct Author Payments**
- Readers pay authors directly
- Authors keep 100% of sales
- Authors manage their own payment methods
- Platform provides the marketplace only

**Option 2: Platform-Managed Payments**
- Platform collects payments on behalf of authors
- Platform takes a commission (e.g., 10-15%)
- Authors receive regular payouts
- Platform handles all payment processing and disputes

Payment features will be implemented once the model is chosen.

## Getting Help

### Common Questions

**I forgot my password**
- We use Google Sign-In, so use your Google account password
- Reset through Google if needed

**My book isn't showing up**
- Check if it's been approved by an admin
- Make sure it's marked as "Published" in your dashboard
- Contact support if issues persist

**How do I contact an author?**
- Visit the author's profile page
- Use the contact information provided
- Or use the "Request Hard Copy" feature

**Can I get a refund?**
- Contact the platform administrator
- Refund policies are handled case-by-case

### Support

Need more help?
- Email: support@kalenjinbooks.com
- Check the FAQ page for common questions
- Contact your administrator for platform-specific issues
